\textit{Dissertation research}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{SRT:} Developed a statistical framework integrating spatial TCR-seq and spatial transcriptomics (earth-mover's distance + Gaussian-mixture FDR control); identified 878 genes coupled with T-cell clonal diversity in glioblastoma, enabling biomarker discovery in the tumor microenvironment.
  \item \textbf{Opioid use disorder} (with multi-site collaborators): Developed Bayesian stochastic block model clustering for 900+ genetically diverse rats, classifying heroin-use-disorder vulnerable, intermediate, and resilient phenotypes and revealing sex-specific subgroups within the vulnerable phenotype (\textit{American Journal of Psychiatry}, 2025). Extending the framework to link behavioral subgroups with brain-region gene expression.
  \item \textbf{SCOPE:} Built an R Shiny app implementing this clustering framework (modularity-initialized Gibbs sampling with posterior uncertainty quantification); adopted by 5+ labs in the USA, Italy, and the UK.
\end{itemize}
\textit{Collaborative projects}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{DNA methylation:} Co-led a genome-wide EWAS (Illumina EPIC; 802,978 CpGs; $n = 324$ postmenopausal women) identifying 736 differentially methylated loci between Native Hawaiian and White women, associated with liver disease pathways (61\% of genes), DASH diet adherence, and adiponectin (\textit{Scientific Reports}, 2025; co-first author).
  \item \textbf{Comparative oncology:} Performed statistical analyses of clinical, histopathologic, and gene expression data from 75 dogs with soft tissue sarcoma; higher FOXP3$^+$ regulatory T-cell infiltration was associated with shorter progression-free intervals, and tumors resembled human undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma, supporting canine sarcoma as a preclinical model (\textit{Cancers}, 2025).
\end{itemize}

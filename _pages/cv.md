Work experience
======
* Summers 2025 & 2026: Biostatistics Intern
  * Abbott Nutrition, Columbus, OH
  * Analyzed a propensity-matched NHANES cohort (n = 6,508; 2011-2018): adults consuming ≥1.2 g/kg/day protein had 1.2% greater lean soft tissue (p < 0.001), independent of physical activity, supporting protein guidelines for healthy aging
  * Owned the full analysis lifecycle: cohort definition, statistical method selection, confounder adjustment, and inference; communicated results to scientific stakeholders and leadership
  * Accepted for poster presentation at ICFSR 2026 (Washington, DC)
  * Completed and presented an individual participant data (IPD) meta-analysis across 14 clinical trials evaluating intervention effects on key health outcomes

* May 2022 - Present: Graduate Research Associate
  * The Ohio State University, Comprehensive Cancer Center; Pelotonia Institute for Immuno-Oncology
  * Developed SRT, a statistical framework integrating spatial TCR-seq and transcriptomics (earth-mover's distance + Gaussian-mixture FDR) that identified 878 genes coupled with T-cell diversity in glioblastoma, a biomarker-discovery method for the tumor microenvironment
  * Developed a Bayesian stochastic block model framework to identify behavioral subgroups in a 900+ subject opioid use disorder study; linked subgroups to brain-region gene expression, with ongoing extension to multivariate phenotype-expression association
  * Built SCOPE, an R Shiny app implementing the Bayesian stochastic block model clustering framework (modularity-initialized Gibbs sampling with posterior uncertainty quantification), adopted by 5+ labs across the USA and international partners (Italy, UK)
  * Engineered a deep learning + feature selection pipeline on 60,000-feature TCGA-BRCA RNA-seq data to identify breast cancer biomarkers across multiple populations
  * Co-led a genome-wide EWAS (Illumina EPIC; 802,978 CpGs; n = 324 postmenopausal women) identifying 736 differentially methylated loci between Native Hawaiian and White women; linked to liver disease pathways (61% of genes), DASH diet adherence, and adiponectin; published co-first author in *Scientific Reports*, 2025

* Aug 2019 - Jul 2021: Statistical Programmer

  * Selected and implemented statistical methods under study analysis plans (SAPs), delivering CDISC-compliant SAS/R programming (SDTM, ADaM, TFL) across three clinical trials, including the pivotal Phase III study for Kisqali (ribociclib)
  * Built reporting databases supporting regulatory submissions across solid-tumor oncology and respiratory therapeutic areas

* Aug 2021 - May 2022: Graduate Teaching Associate

* Aug - Sep 2018: Research Intern

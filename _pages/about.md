I'm a PhD candidate in Biostatistics at [The Ohio State University](https://cph.osu.edu/), working with the [Pelotonia Institute for Immuno-Oncology](https://cancer.osu.edu/) at the OSU Comprehensive Cancer Center. My research develops statistical frameworks for multi-modal biological data, with applications spanning immuno-oncology and the neurogenomics of opioid use disorder. I work at the intersection of Bayesian methods, genomics, and machine learning, and I'm equally at home writing CDISC-compliant SAS/R programs for a Phase III clinical trial submission as I am designing a Gibbs sampler from scratch.

My path here started with a B.S. (Hons.) in Statistics at St. Xavier's College, Calcutta University, followed by an M.S. in Statistics at the University of Delhi, and about two years as a Statistical Programmer at Novartis Healthcare in Hyderabad, where I supported the pivotal Phase III trial for Kisqali (ribociclib) and other oncology and respiratory studies. That regulatory, trial-design grounding is a big part of why I think about biostatistics the way I do: methods should hold up under real scrutiny, not just look good in simulation.

At OSU, I've built:
* **SRT**, a statistical framework that integrates spatial TCR-seq and transcriptomics to identify biomarkers in the glioblastoma tumor microenvironment
* **SCOPE**, an R Shiny app implementing a Bayesian stochastic block model for clustering, now used by 5+ labs across the US, Italy, and the UK
* A genome-wide EWAS linking DNA methylation differences to liver disease pathways and diet, published co-first author in *Scientific Reports* (Nature Portfolio)

Alongside my PhD research, I've spent two summers as a Biostatistics Intern at Abbott Nutrition, analyzing NHANES cohort data on protein intake and lean body mass, and leading an individual participant data meta-analysis across 14 clinical trials.

I currently serve as Student Representative for the Columbus Chapter of the American Statistical Association, and previously spent a year as President of the Statistics & Biostatistics Graduate Student Association at OSU, leading a 100+ member organization.

Co-first author. Genome-wide EWAS (Illumina EPIC; 802,978 CpGs; n = 324 postmenopausal women) identifying 736 differentially methylated loci between Native Hawaiian and White women, with 61% of associated genes linked to liver disease pathways, as well as to DASH diet adherence and adiponectin.

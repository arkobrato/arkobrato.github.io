The TAP (transporter associated with antigen processing) complex, including the TAP2 subunit, plays a key role in presenting viral antigens to the immune system. Herpes simplex virus 1 (HSV-1) evades this immune surveillance in part through its ICP47 protein, which inhibits TAP function. This preprint identifies TAP2 protein variants that resist ICP47-mediated inhibition, pointing toward a mechanism by which antigen presentation could be preserved despite viral immune evasion.

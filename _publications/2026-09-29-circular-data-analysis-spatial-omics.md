Spatial omics technologies often generate data with circular or angular structure (for example, orientation or directional relationships between cells in tissue) that standard linear statistical methods are not designed to handle correctly. This preprint develops and applies circular data analysis methods tailored to spatial omics data, addressing a methodological gap in how directional information is analyzed in this emerging data type.

*This is ongoing work from my time in the Chung lab; a fuller summary will be added once the manuscript is further along.*

Companion work to our TAP2/ICP47 preprint, this study uses multiplexed functional analysis to characterize how TAP2 variants regulate MHC-I cell surface abundance, a key step in antigen presentation to the immune system. The analysis also revealed that overexpression of PLK1 (a cell-cycle regulatory kinase) downregulates antigen presentation, suggesting a previously underappreciated link between cell-cycle regulation and immune surveillance.

*I contributed statistical analysis to this collaborative project in the Parvin lab.*

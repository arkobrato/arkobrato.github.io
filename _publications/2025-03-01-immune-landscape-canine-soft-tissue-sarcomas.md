Performed statistical analyses of clinical, histopathologic, and gene expression data from 75 dogs with soft tissue sarcoma. Higher FOXP3+ regulatory T-cell infiltration was associated with shorter progression-free intervals; tumors resembled human undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma, supporting canine sarcoma as a preclinical model.

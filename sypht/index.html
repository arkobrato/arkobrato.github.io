The shift

This isn’t somebody else’s problem anymore.

For years, syphilis campaigns talked to gay and bisexual men. That still matters. But in Central Ohio the picture has shifted, and more straight men are getting it and passing it on without knowing.

Know the signs

Painless doesn’t mean harmless.

Most guys miss it. The first sign usually doesn’t hurt, and it goes away on its own. But the infection doesn’t go away. It just goes quiet.

About 3 weeks after sex

A sore

A firm, round sore that usually doesn’t hurt. On the penis, anus, mouth or lips. Heals in 3 to 6 weeks, even with no treatment.

Weeks to months later

A rash

A rash that doesn’t itch, often on the palms of your hands or soles of your feet. Can come with fever, swollen glands, sore throat, patchy hair loss or feeling run-down.

Months to years

Hidden

No signs at all. You feel fine. But it’s still in your body, and in the first year you can still pass it on.

Years later

Damage

Without treatment it can harm the brain, heart, nerves and bones. Damage that’s done can’t be undone.

See a doctor today if you have sudden changes in your vision, hearing loss, ringing in your ears, a bad headache, or confusion. Syphilis can reach the eyes, ears and brain at any stage. Tell them you want a syphilis test.

Testing

Quick. Private. No big deal.

A blood test

A quick blood draw or a finger prick. Some clinics have a rapid test with results in about 15 minutes. Ask for an HIV test at the same time.

Or test at home

An FDA-authorized finger-prick home test gives a result in 15 minutes. If it’s positive, you still need a clinic visit to confirm and get treated. If you’ve had syphilis before, skip the home test and go to a clinic.

It’s confidential

Your results are private health information. Medicaid and most insurance cover testing, and public clinics offer low-cost or free tests.

Timing matters. A blood test can miss a very new infection in the first few weeks. If you think you were exposed recently, test now and again in about 6 weeks. If a partner was just diagnosed, ask about getting treated today, even if your test is negative.
Treatment

Catch it early.
One shot. Done.

Syphilis is cured with a penicillin shot. If it’s caught in the first year, one shot is usually all it takes. If it’s been longer, or nobody knows how long, it’s one shot a week for three weeks. Finish all three.

After your shot

  • No sex for 7 days after treatment, and until any sores have fully healed.
  • Make sure your partners get tested and treated, or you can catch it right back.
  • Get a follow-up blood test at 6 and 12 months to make sure it worked.
  • Being cured doesn’t make you immune. You can get it again.
  • Allergic to penicillin? Tell the clinic. There are other options for most men.
Protect your circle

Testing is how you look out for your people.

Your partner

If you test positive, anyone you’ve had sex with in the last few months needs a test and treatment too. Don’t want to have that talk? The health department can let them know anonymously. They won’t say your name.

Your baby

A pregnant woman with syphilis can pass it to her baby. That can cause stillbirth, infant death or lifelong health problems. If your partner is pregnant, you both should get tested. Treating you protects her from getting it again.

Your health

Syphilis sores make it easier to get or pass on HIV. Get tested for both. If you’re at risk for HIV, ask about PrEP, a daily pill or shot that prevents it.

Lower your risk

Use condoms every time, for oral, vaginal and anal sex. They help a lot, but only protect the skin they cover. Fewer partners and regular testing do the rest.

Real talk

Myths vs. facts

MYTHOnly gay guys get syphilis.

FACTAbout 1 in 3 cases in Central Ohio are now in men who have sex with women. Syphilis doesn’t check who you sleep with.

MYTHThe sore went away, so I’m fine.

FACTThe sore heals on its own, but the infection stays. It moves to the next stage and can still be passed on.

MYTHI’d know if I had it.

FACTMany men never notice a sign. The sore is often painless and can be hidden inside the mouth or anus. A test is the only way to know.

MYTHOral sex is safe.

FACTSyphilis spreads through contact with a sore during oral, vaginal or anal sex. Oral counts.

MYTHI had it once, so I can’t get it again.

FACTBeing cured doesn’t protect you. You can get syphilis again, which is why partners need treatment too.

MYTHTreatment means weeks of shots.

FACTCaught early, it’s usually one shot. That’s the whole point of testing now.

MYTHYou can get it from a toilet seat.

FACTNo. Not from toilet seats, doorknobs, pools, hot tubs, shared clothes or forks.
